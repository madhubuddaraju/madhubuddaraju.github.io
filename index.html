I build data pipelines, models and the pages that show whether they work.

Nine projects, each built end to end: getting the data, modeling it in SQL, testing its quality, analysis or machine learning, and a live page with the results. Eight use real public data, from live subway feeds and gas prices to Medicare and FDA records; one uses deliberately messy synthetic data. The code and the evaluation for every one are public.

Projects

Ask the Drug Label

Ask a question about a prescription drug and get the passages from its FDA label that answer it, with an open-source language model that cites them or says it can't answer.

Finds the right label section for 97% of test questions written after tuning was finished. A two-stage check refuses 97% of questions the labels can't answer.

Python, BM25, sentence embeddings, Qwen2.5 on CPU, NLI grading, transformers.js

Subway Line Watch

Spots NYC subway delays as they form: every train's live position goes through Kafka and Spark Structured Streaming, which flags service gaps and bunched trains platform by platform.

2,152 train intervals measured across 25 lines in the first 20-minute session, 81% within the schedule standard. Gap alerts go out before the late train arrives.

Apache Kafka, Spark Structured Streaming (stateful, watermarks, timeouts), GTFS-realtime, Docker

Open the live boardSource codeStreams every hour

Pitstop

A phone page for when you're somewhere new: tap Gas or Food. It lists restaurants open near you with closing times and NYC health grades, explains unfamiliar dishes, and shows nearby stations with this week's local gas price.

171 dishes across 43 cuisines, each with ingredients and how it's made, matched from 160 OpenStreetMap cuisine tags. Gas prices refresh weekly for 29 US areas.

JavaScript, OpenStreetMap Overpass, opening-hours parsing, EIA and NYC Open Data, Python, GitHub Actions

Open on your phoneSource codePrices refresh every Tuesday

Hospital Quality Warehouse

Helps people choose a hospital: enter a ZIP code and a reason for care, and see nearby hospitals ranked on the Medicare measures that matter for it. Built as a tested dbt warehouse run by Dagster.

5,419 hospitals, 30 measures, and rating history rebuilt from 25 past releases through a dbt snapshot. A watch list flags 182 hospitals.

dbt (contracts, unit tests, snapshots), Dagster, DuckDB, SQL, CMS and Census APIs

Find a hospitalSource codeRefreshes every Monday

Consumer Complaint Intelligence

Reads 53,000 complaints filed with the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau: routes each one to a product, finds form letters, tracks rising issues and flags urgent harm.

Routes 42% of complaints automatically at 93% accuracy. A tuned keyword model beat fine-tuned DistilBERT, and runs in the browser from a 4 MB file.

Python, scikit-learn, DistilBERT, topic modeling, JavaScript

Try itSource code

Hospital Readmission Risk

Predicts which diabetic patients will be back in hospital within 30 days, so a care team with limited time calls the right people first.

Flagging the riskiest 20% of discharges catches 39% of readmissions, twice what random selection would, with calibrated probabilities and checks by age, sex and race.

PySpark, MySQL, LightGBM, PyTorch, MLflow, FastAPI, Docker

Open the resultsSource code

Respiratory Disease Surveillance

Weekly flu and COVID-19 hospital admissions for every US state from CDC data, with outbreak alerts and one-to-four-week forecasts.

Forecasts cut error by about 40% against a naive baseline; alerts fired a median 7 weeks before each state's peak.

Python, DuckDB, SQL, LightGBM quantile models, GitHub Actions

Open the dashboardSource codeRefreshes every Friday

Climate and Emissions Tracker

Combines NOAA and NASA temperature records, Mauna Loa CO₂ and emissions for 218 countries in one warehouse, rebuilt every week.

Warming of 0.21 °C per decade since 1975, and 41 countries that grew their economy while cutting CO₂.

Python, DuckDB, SQL, data quality checks, GitHub Actions

Open the dashboardSource codeRefreshes every Monday

E-commerce Data Pipeline

Turns messy order exports into a star-schema warehouse, then answers the questions a shop owner asks: who buys again, which channels pay off, which products sell.

19 quality checks on every run. Each rejected row is logged with its reason, and revenue reconciles at every layer. Built on deliberately messy synthetic data.

Python, DuckDB, SQL, star schema, cohort and RFM analysis
